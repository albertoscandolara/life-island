The 730 is the simplified income tax return for employees and pensioners in Italy. It always covers the year before: the 730/{{ filingYear() }} declares the income and the expenses of {{ taxYear() }}. Your employer or pension body settles it in your pay: a refund arrives in the payslip, tax due is withheld from it.

Who files it

  • Employees and pensioners, and people with similar income (collaborators, unemployment benefit, land and buildings income).
  • Without an employer that pays you in July (sostituto d'imposta), you can still file it: a refund then comes from the Agenzia delle Entrate, tax due is paid with an F24.
  • Business income or VAT number activity needs the Modello Redditi instead.

How it works, year by year

  1. Through the year: keep invoices, receipts and the proof that you paid them traceably. This page files them by year.
  2. By March: your employer sends the Certificazione Unica (CU); doctors, pharmacies, banks, insurers and schools send their data to the Agenzia delle Entrate.
  3. End of April (30 April in 2026): the precompilato is ready on the Agenzia's site, already filled with that data.
  4. From mid May (15 May in 2026): accept it, change it, or have a CAF or commercialista do it. Accepted as it is, the Agenzia does not check the documents of the expenses already in it.
  5. 30 September: last day to send it.
  6. July (pensioners August or September): the refund is in your pay; tax due is withheld, in instalments up to November if you ask.
  7. Made a mistake? A corrective 730 (integrativo) by 25 October if it raises your refund; otherwise the Modello Redditi.

What goes in it

  • Your details and family: dependants, who pays you in July.
  • Income: land and buildings (quadri A and B), work and pension (C, from the CU), other income (D).
  • Expenses (quadro E): where this page helps. Section I, expenses giving back 19% (health, vets, school, sport, mortgage interest, insurance, estate agent); section II, deductible contributions (pension funds, domestic workers); sections III and IV, home bonuses (renovation, furniture, energy) over 10 years.
  • Taxes already paid and credits (F, G), and rent bonuses for tenants.

Detrazione or deduzione

  • A detrazione lowers the tax: 19% of a health expense, 50% of a renovation. This page estimates these.
  • A deduzione (onere deducibile) lowers the income the tax is computed on: pension fund contributions up to €5,164.57, domestic workers' contributions up to €1,549.37, donations, alimony.
  • A detrazione can only take back tax you paid: if your tax is lower, the rest is lost, and so are that year's home-bonus instalments.

What you get back

  • The settlement (730-3) compares the tax withheld from your pay during the year with the tax due after the detrazioni: the difference is your refund, or what you owe.
  • The detrazioni estimated on this page are roughly how much the refund grows thanks to your expenses.

Limits to keep in mind

  • Each kind has its own cap and rate: no cap on health but the first €129.11 never counts; €550 for vets; €96,000 per home for renovations, over 10 years. The table below lists them all.
  • Above €120,000 of income the 19% detrazioni shrink, to nothing at €240,000 (health and mortgage interest excepted).
  • Above €75,000 (from the 2025 expenses), all your detrazioni together are capped at €14,000 (up to €100,000) or €8,000 (above), times 0.50 with no children, 0.70 with one, 0.85 with two, 1 with three or more. Health expenses, and loans, policies and renovations from before 2025, stay outside.
  • Pay traceably: card, bank transfer or cheque. Cash is fine only for medicines, medical devices and the public health service. Home bonuses need a bonifico parlante.

Documents to keep

  • The Certificazione Unica of every employer or pension.
  • Every invoice or receipt with your codice fiscale (scontrino parlante at the pharmacy), and its payment proof.
  • Mortgage: the deed, the loan contract and the bank's yearly interest statement.
  • Home bonuses: bonifici parlanti, invoices, permits or the self-declaration, ENEA filing for energy works; condominium statements.
  • Keep them five years after filing: the 730/{{ filingYear() }} until 31 December {{ filingYear() + 5 }}. Home bonus documents, for as long as you take the instalments and five years after.
