Follow the paths you care about; Overview shows the next step of each one under Up next. A session logged on a step starts it, and you mark it done when you can do what it promises (its You will line).

Give a path a Finish by date and Studies tells you, at the pace of your last weeks, whether you will make it. Your weekly goal is shared among the paths you follow.

Every few months (every two for AI, frameworks and infrastructure, every six for the rest) it searches the web for the best recent resources for the stage you are on and the next one. It prefers official documentation, the authors' own material and well-known courses, and only uses addresses it actually saw.

It also checks the resources you pinned and flags the outdated ones, and may propose a step to add or retire. Nothing changes until you pin, dismiss, accept or decline. With the tool off, every step keeps its hand-picked resources and what you pinned.

Your accounts and notes are never sent. Without a Claude API key, or with the tool off in Settings → AI tools, the plan comes from Life Island's own rules: an exam first, then the steps you are studying, then the next step of each path, sized to your weekly goal.

  • Short and often beats long and rare. Three sessions of 50 minutes in a week stick better than one afternoon.
  • Build, do not only read. Each stage has build and practice steps: they are where the learning happens.
  • Write what you learned. A line in a step's notes is what you reread before an exam or an interview.
  • Finish before you start. A step you are studying comes before a new one.
