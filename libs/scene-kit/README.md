# scene-kit

What the 3D scenes share (the island hub, the Cars garage, the Houses of Home
and the Dreams sky): loading a kit of Blender models (`loadSceneKit`), placing
a copy painted with the scene's own materials (`placeKitModel`), and reading
design tokens as colours WebGL understands (`toHex`, `luminance`).

The kits are built by the scripts in `tools/scene-models` and
`tools/island-models`.
